CircuitKit brand kit

Light variants contain dark ink for light surfaces. Dark variants contain light ink for dark surfaces.
Open index.html for previews and usage guidelines. Icons with maskable in their name include extra safe space for operating-system crops.
All logos are supplied as outlined SVG and transparent PNG. Social compositions also include WebP. Typeface files are Geist Sans and Geist Mono, licensed under OFL.txt.

Source repository: https://github.com/crafter-lab/circuitkit
